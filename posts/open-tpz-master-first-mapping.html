Inspect your first mapping with Open Tpz Master

Mustafa Alp · Draft tutorial · Updated 9 October 2026

Release View v0.1.0

The most useful first exercise is small: explain how a single target field gets its value.

The scenario

Imagine reviewing a synthetic employee mapping before handing it over to another consultant. You want to understand how a target display name is assembled.

Illustrative example: source fields FirstName and LastName contribute to DisplayName. This example is not a supplied TPZ file or a tested execution result.

Inspect the mapping

  1. Open your TPZ export and choose the relevant message mapping.
  2. Locate the target field you want to explain.
  3. Follow the displayed brick tree and identify the contributing source fields.
  4. Note any visible concatenation, defaulting or conditional functions.
  5. Write down questions about empty values and repeated records.

Write a useful result

Your output is a short explanation of the mapping, a list of its inputs and any unresolved questions. Distinguish what the graph shows from what you still need to test.

Validate your interpretation

Compare with the original mapping and appropriate test cases. Include missing inputs and repeated records where relevant. A visual inspection alone does not prove how the mapping executes.
