A simple structure for technical project notes

Mustafa Alp · 6 October 2026

A technical note is most useful when a reader can understand the context and follow the reasoning behind a decision.

Sample article — this is an editorial template, not a description of a completed client project.

1. Describe the problem

Explain the original behavior and why it needed to change. State the relevant constraints, such as message size, delivery requirements or available adapters.

2. Explain the approach

Describe the options you considered and the reason for the selected approach. Use anonymized examples to illustrate the important details.

3. Record the validation

Write down what you checked and what the result tells you. Distinguish a working proof of concept from a production implementation.

4. Capture the lessons

Close with what you would reuse and what you would change next time.
